2.5mm of bone

  1. 2.5 mm

    What was there

    Under the maxillary sinus, at the upper second molar, 2.5 mm of bone was left. An implant would be anchored in bone along only a quarter of its length.

  2. 7.5 mm

    What had to be gained

    A 10 mm implant needs the sinus floor raised by 7.5 mm without tearing the membrane that lines it. Dentists call this an extreme sinus lift.

  3. 10 mm

    What was done

    10 mm implant placed. Cases like this are planned on a CT scan before surgery and explained to the patient on the same image.

What people usually ask

How much does it cost?

It depends on the case, so we don’t publish fixed prices. At the first visit you get the plan with the price of each option, before any treatment starts.

I’m in pain right now. Can you see me soon?

Message us on WhatsApp or book with “I’m in pain” ticked. We’ll tell you when we can see you. Patients with acute pain write on Google that they were seen the next day.

I’m scared of dentists.

It’s what reviews mention most. Before starting we explain what we’ll do and why, and nothing starts until it’s clear to you.

Another clinic told me nothing can be done.

Bring your X-rays or CT scan if you have them. We’ll look and tell you honestly what can and can’t be done.

Can I book with a specific dentist?

Yes, say so when you book and we’ll confirm who will see you.

Which languages do you speak?

English, Spanish and Russian.

Where can I park?

There is parking near the clinic, and from the centre it’s a walk across Torres de Serranos.
